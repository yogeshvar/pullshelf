v0.1.1

Renamed to Pullshelf

  • The app, Homebrew cask and website are now called Pullshelf (it was Perch).

v0.1.0 (as Perch)

First public release

  • Pull-down shelf with terminal, clipboard history, files and notes.
  • Two-finger pull at the top edge, or ⌘⇧L.
  • Light and dark themes, collapsible and resizable panes.
  • Menu bar settings: launch at login, hide the icon, quit.
  • Homebrew cask and direct download.
