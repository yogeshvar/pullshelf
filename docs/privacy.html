Privacy

Your data stays yours

Pullshelf has no telemetry, no accounts and no cloud sync. It makes no network calls of its own.

What is stored, and where

Clipboard history, notes and file references are kept in a local database under ~/Library/Application Support/Pullshelf. Notes are also mirrored as Markdown files in that folder. Delete the folder to remove everything.

Permissions

Accessibility is used only to detect the two-finger pull at the top screen edge. Pullshelf reads scroll events for that and nothing else.

This website

It sets no cookies and loads no third-party fonts or trackers. It uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights, served from this same site, to count page views and measure load speed in aggregate; they do not use cookies or track you across sites. Your theme choice is kept in your browser's local storage. The app itself has no analytics.
