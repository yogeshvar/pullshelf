Download Pullshelf-0.1.1-arm64.zip

Unzip and drag Pullshelf to Applications.

First launch

Pullshelf is not notarized yet. Open it once, then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway (older macOS: right-click, Open). For the pull gesture, allow Pullshelf under Privacy & Security, Accessibility. You can skip that and use ⌘⇧L instead.
